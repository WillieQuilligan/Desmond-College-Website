Our History

Desmond College was founded in 1968. It began as a small college with only a few departments and just over 300 students. Today, it has grown into a large and respected college with more than 12,000 students.


Over the years, the college has expanded its courses, improved its facilities, and built a strong reputation for teaching and research. We continue to focus on academic excellence, innovation, and student success.

Key Milestones

Vision and Mission

The values that guide Desmond College.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be recognised as a leading centre of learning and research. We want to inspire students to achieve their goals and make a positive difference in the world.


Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a welcoming and supportive learning environment. We help students develop the knowledge, skills, and confidence they need for successful careers and meaningful lives.

Our Main Goals

What we aim to achieve in the years ahead.

Research and Innovation: We will continue to invest in research and new ideas across many different fields.


Online Learning: We are expanding our online courses to make education more flexible and accessible.


Industry Partnerships: We work closely with employers to provide work experience and career opportunities.


Sustainability: We are committed to reducing our environmental impact and becoming carbon neutral.


Inclusion: We aim to create a diverse and welcoming community for all students and staff.


Global Connections: We continue to build partnerships with universities around the world.

Our Facilities

Modern facilities to support learning and student life.

Laboratories: Our science and technology labs provide excellent spaces for practical learning and research.


Library: The college library offers a wide range of books, journals, study spaces, and online resources.


Sports Centre: Students can enjoy a modern gym, swimming pool, sports hall, and outdoor playing fields.


Research Centres: Our research centres support innovation in areas such as science, engineering, and technology.
