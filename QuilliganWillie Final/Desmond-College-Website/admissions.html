Applying to Desmond College

Applications are now open. The normal CAO closing date is 1 February 2025.

Desmond College welcomes applications from students of all backgrounds. Undergraduate applications are made through the CAO, while postgraduate applications are made directly through our website.


Entry Requirements

Applicants normally need a Leaving Certificate with six subjects. Some courses, such as Science and Engineering, require higher grades in Maths and science subjects. International applicants must also show English language proficiency.


How to Apply

First, choose the course that interests you and check its entry requirements. Next, submit your application through the CAO. After applying, upload any required documents and check your email regularly for updates. If you receive an offer, you must accept it through the CAO before registering as a student.


Required Documents

  • CAO application confirmation
  • Certified exam results
  • Valid photo ID
  • Recent passport photograph
  • English language certificate (if required)
  • Personal statement
