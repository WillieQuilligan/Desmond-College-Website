About Desmond College

Desmond College was founded in 1968. Since then, it has grown into one of Ireland's leading colleges. We offer a wide range of courses and provide a supportive learning environment for all students.


Our aim is to help students achieve their goals through quality teaching, modern facilities, and exciting opportunities both inside and outside the classroom.

What We Offer

Desmond College provides everything students need to learn, grow, and succeed.

Academic Programmes: We offer courses in Science, Technology, Arts, Business, and many other subject areas.


Research Opportunities: Students and staff work together on important research in areas such as health, technology, and the environment.


Student Support: Our support services include academic guidance, counselling, and career advice.


Campus Life: Students can join clubs, societies, and sports teams, making college life enjoyable and rewarding.

Desmond College by the Numbers

A quick look at our college community.

Over 12,000 students study at Desmond College each year. We offer more than 120 programmes and have over 900 members of academic staff. Our graduates enjoy a 96% employment rate, and students can take part in more than 60 clubs and societies.
